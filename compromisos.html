Impact Audit

This phase is not just visual; it is a strategic diagnosis. I will analyze how the NGO's current identity is failing to communicate its mission. 94% of first digital impressions are related to design, and a professional audit identifies the barriers preventing donors from trusting the organization.

Brand Architecture

Building a system, not just a logo. Brand architecture ensures the organization has a consistent voice across all touchpoints. A well-designed system reduces production costs for materials by 60% and generates an immediate perception of institutional professionalism.

Visual UX Optimization

Distinguishing decorative design from functional design. I will apply principles of legibility and visual hierarchy to ensure that critical messages (such as requests for help or rights information) are consumable by users with varying cognitive or technical abilities.

Autonomy Delivery

My goal is for the NGO to be self-sufficient. I will deliver smart templates and usage guides that allow non-design staff to create high-quality content. This ensures the sustainability of the visual impact long after my intervention has ended.

Critical Impact Filter

I will annually select projects where interactive design can solve life-or-death problems or access to fundamental rights. It's not about "doing favors," but about applying high-level consultancy where social need is most urgent.

Field UX Research

Social design is not done from a desk. I will move to the real context of users (shelters, community centers, rural areas) to observe behaviors and co-create solutions born from reality, not academic assumptions.

Inclusive Prototyping

I will develop solutions that comply with international WCAG standards. Interactive design must be universal; if a digital tool cannot be used by a person with a disability, then the design has failed in its civic mission.

Results Reporting

Impact must be measured. I will deliver reports demonstrating how the design intervention improved usability rates or the organization's reach, closing the commitment cycle with transparency and real data.
