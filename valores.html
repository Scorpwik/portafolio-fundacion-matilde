Critical Empathy

Why is it important in my civic commitment?
Empathy in the context of community service transcends superficial compassion. For an interactive media designer, empathy is a working methodology. Without it, we risk imposing solutions that do not adapt to the cultural or social reality of the community, wasting efforts and resources.

How did I apply it in my service?
At the Matilde Foundation, listening to the mothers meant suspending my professional assumptions. Listening sessions revealed that the needs perceived by the design team did not always coincide with the real needs. I used participant observation tools to adapt the sexual health project, ensuring the language was dignified and accessible.

Supportive Efficiency

Why is it important in my civic commitment?
Efficiency in the context of an NGO is not an operational luxury it is an ethical obligation. Every resource a professional wastes due to a lack of technical rigor is a resource that stops reaching a direct beneficiary. As a professional citizen, my duty is to optimize existing social capital.

How did I apply it in my service?
I diagnosed that the water filters were not working due to a simple but ignored technical incompatibility. By recovering them and managing the configuration of donated hardware for the educational area, I managed to multiply the foundation's impact without it having to invest additional funds.

Pedagogical Responsibility

Why is it important in my civic commitment?
Responsibility goes beyond fulfilling schedules. It implies understanding that we are role models. Educating the environment in civic values is the only way to ensure that social change is sustainable in the long term, breaking the cycles of indifference that affect our city.

How did I apply it in my service?
Beyond my technical tasks, I dedicated daily time to guiding children in courtyard maintenance and waste management. It was a lesson in patience and consistency, teaching them that caring for Matilde House's space is an act of respect for themselves and their community.
